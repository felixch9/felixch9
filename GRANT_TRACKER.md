## Priority — prepare now

### NEH Fellowships — up to $60,000

- Funder: National Endowment for the Humanities
- Next listed application availability: January 15, 2027
- Deadline: April 14, 2027
- Funding: up to $60,000 ($5,000/month), 6–12 months
- Applicant type: individuals
- NEH explicitly encourages independent and junior scholars.
- Current program emphasis: American history and culture / Western civilization.
- Important constraint: award period requires full-time project work, so employment/leave feasibility must be resolved before accepting an award.
- Potential fit: an American-history/cultural-landscape project producing a peer-reviewed article, digital scholarly resource, or book-length research trajectory.
- Status: **serious future individual target; build a competitive research record and project now**.

Official page: https://www.neh.gov/grants/research/fellowships

### SEAC Public Outreach Grant — up to $5,000

- Funder: Southeastern Archaeological Conference
- Purpose: public awareness of archaeology in the Southeast.
- Applicant pool is broad; projects must have a Southeastern connection.
- The most recently posted cycle awarded/requested $1,000–$5,000 and closed January 12, 2026.
- The next cycle must be verified when SEAC posts updated dates.

- Status: **excellent small-grant target when the next cycle opens**.

Official page: https://www.southeasternarchaeology.org/Public-Outreach-Grant

- No experience or research document required for the Student/Early Career category.

- Status: **verify degree timing/specialization and apply if eligible**.

## Institutional / collaborator route

### NPS American Battlefield Protection Program — Preservation Planning Grants

- Typical awards: $20,000–$200,000.
- No matching funds required.
- Eligible applicants include educational institutions, state/local/Tribal governments, public entities, and nonprofits.
- Individual applicant route: no.
- Typical annual NOFO window: roughly mid-December through mid-March.
- Projects may include archaeological survey, research, documentation, preservation planning, interpretation, and related work for sites of armed conflict on American soil.
- Potential fit: a university/SCIAA/local-government/nonprofit partnership on a qualifying South Carolina military or conflict landscape, with Felix contributing GIS/research work.
- Status: **build project concept + institutional partner before the next NOFO**.

Official page: https://www.nps.gov/orgs/2287/preservation-planning-grants.htm

### NEH Collections Stewardship

- Applicant: organizations.

- Status: **institutional collaboration opportunity**.

Official page: https://www.neh.gov/program/collections-stewardship

- Status: **watch next cycle; likely institutional/local-government/nonprofit route rather than personal money**.

# Funding strategy

1. **$0–$5k stage:** win/participate in outreach, professional-society, travel, microgrant, or local/institutional opportunities while generating real outputs.
2. **$5k–$50k stage:** join institutional projects as GIS/research personnel or co-investigator where permitted; pursue individual humanities/research funding where eligible.
3. **$20k–$200k stage:** develop institutional heritage/archaeology projects with university, nonprofit, SHPO, museum, or government partners.
4. **Graduate/PhD stage:** unlock Wenner-Gren, AIA technology grants, NSF archaeology pathways, PI/co-PI opportunities, and larger independent research programs.
5. **Never chase money unrelated to the research identity just to claim a grant.** Funding should compound the research program.
