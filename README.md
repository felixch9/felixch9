# Felix Collazo

**Archaeology × GIS × Remote Sensing × Reproducible Research**

I am building a long-term body of work at the intersection of archaeology, anthropology, geospatial analysis, cultural-resource management, open research, and applied computing.

My goal is simple: produce work that is useful enough to be **tested, reused, cited, and improved by other researchers**.

## Research direction

- Archaeological GIS and spatial analysis
- Southeastern United States archaeology
- Cultural landscapes and historical archaeology
- LiDAR, remote sensing, terrain analysis, and historical-map integration
- Archaeological predictive modeling and uncertainty
- Cultural-resource management and stewardship
- Reproducible/open research using Python, GIS, and public data
- Responsible applications of machine learning to archaeological questions

## Current build

I am developing a reproducible research pipeline for finding important archaeological questions, auditing the literature, locating legal/public datasets, testing geospatial methods, documenting analyses, and converting completed work into papers, maps, software, public scholarship, and conference material.

**Research rule:** no fabricated fieldwork, results, credentials, citations, affiliations, or data. Sensitive archaeological and restricted government information stays restricted.

## Long-term questions

> How can geospatial computation, historical records, remote sensing, archaeological evidence, and reproducible analytical methods help reconstruct and protect cultural landscapes whose traces are difficult to see?

> How do we make archaeological prediction scientifically useful without confusing prediction with explanation or hiding uncertainty?
